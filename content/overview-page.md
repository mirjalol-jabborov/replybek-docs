**Overview** is the first page in the sidebar. It shows what needs you today.

## Get set up

Until your first agent is live, a checklist at the top walks you through the four steps: **Create an agent**, **Add knowledge**, **Test answers in the Playground** and **Publish your agent**. Each step has a button that takes you there. The checklist disappears once everything is done.

## The numbers

@clip overview-page | Waiting customers, AI resolution, conversations and AI messages, then what needs attention.

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for a teammate | How many customers are waiting for a person right now, and the longest wait. |
| Resolved by AI | The share of conversations the AI finished without a person. |
| Conversations | How many conversations started, compared with the previous period. |
| AI messages used | This month's AI replies against your plan's allowance. See [Usage and limits](usage-and-limits). |

Switch the period with **7 days**, **30 days** and **90 days** at the top.

## Needs attention

Conversations handed to your team, longest wait first, with who they're assigned to. **Open** goes straight to the conversation in the [inbox](inbox). When nobody's waiting, it says so.

## Knowledge gaps

Questions the agent couldn't answer, with how often they were asked, and knowledge sources that failed. **Add answer** fixes a gap in one step. See [Unanswered questions](unanswered).

## Your agents and recent activity

The side column lists your agents with their status and knowledge, and a feed of recent changes: agents published, knowledge added, conversations handed off.

At the bottom, **Conversation volume** charts conversations per day. For the full picture, open [Analytics](analytics).
