Every plan starts with a **7-day free trial**. You add a card, nothing is charged for 7 days, and you can cancel any time before then.

## Plans

| | Starter | Growth | Business |

What happens at each limit is explained in [Usage and limits](usage-and-limits).

## Start your free trial

:::steps
1. Open **Settings & billing**, then **Plan & billing**. Or press **Start free trial** on the banner at the top of any page.
2. Pick a plan and press **Start free trial**.
3. Add your card on Polar's secure checkout page. Polar is the payment provider Replybek uses.
4. You come back to Replybek. Within a few seconds the plan shows **Free trial**, with the date the first payment is due.
:::

@clip billing-trial | Choose a plan to start the free trial.

Only the owner of the workspace can start the trial or change the plan.

## Before the trial starts

You can build and try your first agent during setup. After that, until the trial starts, the workspace is **read-only**:

- you can see everything, and test your agent in the [Playground](playground) with up to 30 answers a month,
- your website widget doesn't answer with AI. New chats go to your team in the [inbox](inbox),
- you can't add or change agents, knowledge, integrations or settings.

Starting the trial unlocks everything.

## Change your plan

Open **Plan & billing** and press **Upgrade** or **Switch** on the plan you want. During a trial or a paid subscription the change happens right away, without a new checkout. Polar adjusts the charge for the rest of the billing period. Your limits change immediately.

@clip billing-active | During the trial, switch to Business. It takes effect at once.

## Manage payments

**Manage billing on Polar** opens Polar's customer portal, already signed in, where you can:

- update your card,
- download invoices,
- cancel your subscription.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Free trial | The trial is running. The first payment is due on the date shown. |
| Active | Paid and renewing on the date shown. |
| Payment overdue | The last payment failed. Update your card in the portal to keep your plan. |
| Ends at period end | Cancelled. Everything keeps working until the date shown. |
| Cancelled | The subscription has ended. |

## When a subscription ends

Your agents stop answering with AI and pass new chats to your team, and the workspace becomes read-only again. Nothing is deleted. Choose a plan to continue where you left off.
