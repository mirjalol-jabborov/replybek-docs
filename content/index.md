Replybek works in Uzbek, Russian and English. You teach an AI agent your business, put its chat on your website, and watch every conversation from one inbox.

## How it works

:::cards
- [Teach it your business](knowledge): Import your website, upload documents or write answers. The agent answers only from what you give it.
- [Connect your systems](integrations): Let the agent look up orders or create them in your own system. Every change waits for the customer's Confirm.
- [Put it on your website](install-widget): Paste one line of code. The chat matches your brand and speaks your customer's language.
- [Stay in control](inbox): When a customer needs a person, the conversation lands in your inbox and the AI steps aside.
:::
