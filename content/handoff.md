A conversation is handled by either the AI or a person, never both at once. That way a customer never gets two different answers.

## When the AI hands off

The AI passes a conversation to your team when the customer asks for a person, reports a damaged or wrong item, or your instructions say a teammate must handle it. The customer can also press **Talk to a person** at any time. See [How agents answer](how-agents-answer#when-it-hands-off-to-a-person).

The conversation then shows **Needs human** in the inbox, the AI stops replying, and the customer sees that a teammate has been asked to join. If nobody has the inbox open, your team gets an [email alert](email-alerts).

## Take over

Open the conversation and press **Take over**. The AI pauses, the status becomes **Human handling**, and the conversation is assigned to you. Type your reply and press **Enter**. The customer sees it in their chat right away, marked as coming from your team.

@clip inbox-takeover | Take over a conversation and reply to the customer.

You can take over any conversation, not only the ones that need a person. If the AI is answering and you'd rather reply yourself, press **Take over** to step in.

:::tip
Read **Summary so far** before you reply. It tells you what the customer needs and what the AI already said, so you don't ask them to repeat it.
:::

## Hand back to the AI

When you're done, open the **⋯** menu at the top of the conversation and choose **Hand back to AI**. The AI answers the customer's next messages again. Or [mark it resolved](assign-and-resolve#resolve-a-conversation) if the conversation is finished.

## Who sees what

| | Customer sees | Your team sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI answers | Yes | Yes, with sources |
| Team replies | Yes, with your team's name | Yes |
| Internal notes | No | Yes |
| Events like "took over" | No | Yes |
