---
title: Assign and resolve
description: Decide who handles a conversation, and close it when it's done.
---

## Assign a conversation

In the details panel on the right, pick a teammate under **Assignee**. They see it in their **Mine** view. Choose **Unassigned** to clear it. **Assign to me** in the conversation's **⋯** menu is a shortcut for yourself. Taking over a conversation also assigns it to you.

@clip inbox-assign | Assign to a colleague, hand back to the AI, and resolve.

## Resolve a conversation

When a customer's question is settled, press **Mark resolved** in the details panel, or choose it from the **⋯** menu. The conversation moves to the **Resolved** view. If the customer writes again, it opens again by itself and the AI answers.

To continue a resolved conversation yourself, press **Reopen**.

## Mark as unread

Opening a conversation marks it as read. To come back to it later, choose **Mark as unread** in the **⋯** menu. It gets its dot back in the list.

## The conversation menu

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Assign to me | Makes you the assignee. |
| Hand back to AI | The AI answers new messages again. Shown while a teammate is handling it. |
| Mark resolved / Reopen | Closes the conversation, or opens it again. |
| Mark as unread | Shows it as unread in the list. |
