Each plan has limits on AI messages, agents, team seats, knowledge sources and pages per website. See them side by side in [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing#plans).

## See your usage

**Plan & billing** shows a meter for each limit, and turns it amber when you're close. The box at the bottom of the sidebar always shows this month's AI messages.

## How AI messages are counted

Every reply the AI writes counts as one AI message: in the website widget and in the Playground. Replies from your team don't count.

The allowance is per calendar month and starts again on the 1st.

## When AI messages run out

- The website widget stops answering with AI. Customers can still write; their chats go straight to your team in the [inbox](inbox).
- The Playground stops answering.
- A banner on every page says how long until answers come back.
- Owners and admins get one email that month.

@clip limit-messages | The banner when this month's AI messages are used up.

@shot email-allowance | The email owners and admins get.

Upgrading turns answers back on right away. Otherwise they resume on the 1st of next month.

## Agents and knowledge sources

When all agents or knowledge sources on your plan are in use, **Create agent** and **Add knowledge** explain the limit and point to the plans instead of opening the form.

@clip limit-agents | Starter includes one agent, so creating a second one points to the plans.

Nothing is ever deleted when you're at a limit, or when you move to a smaller plan. If your usage is above a smaller plan's limits, remove some agents or sources before switching to it.

## Seats and pages

- **Seats**: members and open invitations each use one. See [Team](team).
- **Pages per website**: a website source imports pages up to your plan's limit; the rest are left out. See [Websites](websites).
