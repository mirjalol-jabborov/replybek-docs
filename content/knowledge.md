Knowledge is everything an agent may use in its answers. It answers from its knowledge and nothing else, so the quality of its answers depends on it.

Open **Knowledge** in the sidebar. The agent menu at the top picks whose knowledge you're looking at: each agent has its own sources.

## Three kinds of sources

| Source | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| [Website](websites) | Your help center, delivery, returns and product pages. Replybek keeps them in sync. |
| [Files](files) | Documents your team already has: price lists, policies, manuals. PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD or CSV. |
| [Q&A](qa) | Short answers in your own words, for questions the other sources don't answer well. |

Press **Add knowledge**, or one of the three cards at the top of the page, to add a source.

## The sources table

Every source shows its type, readiness, when it was last updated and its size. Search by name, or filter by **Websites**, **Files** or **Q&A**. The count on the right says how many sources are ready.

@clip knowledge-table | The sources table. Click a source to see its details and the pages it read.

Readiness tells you whether the agent can use a source yet:

| Readiness | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Processing | Replybek is reading the content, splitting it into passages and indexing them. Large sites take a few minutes. |
| Ready | The agent can quote it in answers. |
| Failed | It couldn't be read. Open it to see why, then press **Retry**. |

## Source details

Click a source to open its details: what it is, how many passages the agent can quote, and a preview of the first ones. For a website you also see every page it read, with failed pages first, and its [re-sync](websites#keep-it-up-to-date) settings.

Press **Remove source** to stop the agent using it. You can add it again later.

:::note Plan limits
A workspace can have 10 sources on Starter, 50 on Growth and 250 on Business, counted across all agents. A whole website counts as one source. See [Usage and limits](usage-and-limits).
:::

## Find what's missing

Questions the agent couldn't answer show up as [unanswered questions](unanswered) in Analytics and on the [Overview](overview-page). Answering them there adds a Q&A source in one step.
