Open **Analytics** in the sidebar. Choose the period, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**, and one agent or all of them.

@clip analytics | Hover a day for details, change the period, and scroll to the unanswered questions.

## The numbers

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Conversations | Conversations in which a visitor sent at least one message, with the change from the previous period of the same length. |
| Resolved by AI | Conversations the AI handled without a person. |
| Handed to a person | Conversations that were handed to your team at any point. |
| Unanswered questions | Open questions the agent couldn't answer from its knowledge. |

## Conversation volume

One bar per day, split into conversations the AI resolved and those handed to a person. Hover a bar to see that day's numbers. Days are counted in your workspace's time zone, which you set in [Workspace settings](workspace-settings).

## AI resolution vs handoff

The share of conversations the AI resolved compared with the share handed to a person. A rising handoff share usually means customers ask about something the agent's knowledge doesn't cover.

## Unanswered questions

At the bottom of the page: every question the agent couldn't answer, how many times it was asked and when last. Press **Add answer** to teach the agent, or delete a question that doesn't need one. See [Unanswered questions](unanswered).

:::tip A weekly routine
Once a week: answer the top unanswered questions, read a few handed-off conversations, and check the resolution share. See [Best practices](best-practices#review-every-week).
:::
