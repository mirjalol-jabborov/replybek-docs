## Sign in

Go to [app.replybek.uz](https://app.replybek.uz). Sign in with your email and password, or press **Continue with Google**. New here? Press **Create a workspace**, see [Build your first AI agent](first-agent#create-your-account).

## Forgot your password

Press **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your email. If an account exists, you get a link to choose a new password. The link works for 1 hour. Choosing a new password signs you out on your other devices.

@clip account-reset | Ask for a password reset link.

## Your profile

Open the menu with your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Your profile** to change your name and photo. Your teammates see them in the inbox. Customers see your replies under your team's name.

## Language

The dashboard speaks **English**, **Oʻzbekcha** and **Русский**. Switch in the menu with your name. Each teammate chooses their own, and Replybek's emails to you follow it.

@clip account-language | Switch the dashboard to Uzbek, Russian and back to English.

This only changes the dashboard. Your customers' chat follows their own language, see [Widget languages](widget-languages).

## Light and dark

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** in the same menu. System follows your computer's setting.

@clip account-theme | Switch to dark and back.

## Quick search

Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K** on Windows), or **Quick search** at the top, to jump to any page or agent by typing.

@clip account-search | Type "inbox" and press Enter.

## Help

The **?** button at the top opens a short guide to how Replybek works, in five steps. These docs go into each step in detail.

## Sign out

Choose **Sign out** in the menu with your name.
