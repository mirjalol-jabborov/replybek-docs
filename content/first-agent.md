Replybek walks you through a short setup the first time you sign in. It takes about five minutes.

## Create your account

Go to [app.replybek.uz](https://app.replybek.uz) and press **Create a workspace**. Enter your name, email, a password of at least 10 characters and your business name. Or press **Continue with Google** to sign up with your Google account.

@clip signup | Sign up, and the setup opens on its own.

The setup opens right after sign-up. You don't need to look for it: it appears for the owner of a new workspace until the first agent exists.

:::note Skipped it, or left halfway?
**Skip for now** takes you to the dashboard. To pick up where you left off, open [app.replybek.uz/#/onboarding](https://app.replybek.uz/#/onboarding). The **Get set up** checklist on the [Overview](overview-page) page covers the same steps one by one.
:::

Joining a teammate's workspace instead? Use the link from your invitation email. See [Team](team).

## 1. Enter your website

Type your website's address and press **Build my agent**. Replybek opens the site, reads its sitemap and finds your business name, logo and brand colour.

@clip onboarding-website | Replybek reads the website and fills in the name, logo and chat colour.

Check what it found. You can change the business name, pick another chat colour, replace the logo or write a different welcome message. The chat preview on the right updates as you type.

:::tip
Use the address of the site your customers already know. If your help pages live somewhere else, like `help.yourshop.uz`, add that site later from [Knowledge](websites).
:::

## 2. Train the agent

Press **Create my agent**. Replybek reads your pages, splits them into passages and indexes them, then writes a few starter questions. You see the progress as it goes.

How many pages it reads depends on your plan: up to 50 on Starter, 500 on Growth and 5,000 on Business. See [Usage and limits](usage-and-limits).

## 3. Try it

Ask what a customer would ask. The agent answers only from your website, links each answer to the page it came from, and says so when it doesn't know.

@clip onboarding-train | Training takes about a minute. Then the agent answers a real question from the website.

If an answer is wrong or missing, the page probably doesn't say it clearly. You can add a written answer later, see [Q&A answers](qa).

## 4. Go live

Press **Looks good**. Replybek publishes the agent and shows the code for your website. Copy it and paste it before the closing `</body>` tag on every page where the chat should appear.

@clip onboarding-live | The embed code, ready to copy.

:::note Free trial
Before your agent can answer visitors, start the 7-day free trial. You add a card on Polar's secure page, and nothing is charged for 7 days. Until then you can test the agent with 30 free answers. See [Plans and billing](plans-and-billing).
:::

Not ready to edit your site? Press **Go to my dashboard** and come back to the code any time from [Website widget](install-widget).
