Open **Settings & billing**, then **Team**. You see everyone in the workspace, their role and when they joined, and the invitations still waiting.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including the plan and billing. Each workspace has one owner: the person who created it. |
| Admin | Manages agents, knowledge, integrations, settings and the team, and answers conversations. |
| Agent | Answers conversations in the inbox. Teammates with this role start in the [Inbox](inbox). |

## Invite a teammate

:::steps
1. Press **Invite teammate**.
2. Enter their email and choose a role: **Agent** or **Admin**.
3. Press **Send invitation**. They get an email with a link to join. It works for 7 days.
:::

@clip team-invite | Invite a new admin. The invitation waits in the list until they join.

@shot email-invite | The invitation email.

The invited person opens the link, enters their name and a password, and lands in the workspace. If they already have a Replybek account with that email, they sign in to accept instead.

@clip team-join | Joining from the invitation link.

## Manage invitations and members

- **Resend** sends a new link and gives the invitation 7 more days. The old link stops working.
- **×** cancels an invitation.
- The **Role** menu next to a member changes their role.
- **Remove from team**, in the member's **⋯** menu, takes away their access. Conversations they were handling go back to the queue.

@clip team-roles | Resend an invitation, and the menu for a member.

Admins can't remove the owner, and nobody can remove themselves.

:::note Seats
Each plan includes a number of seats: 2 on Starter, 5 on Growth and 15 on Business. Members and open invitations both use a seat. When all seats are taken, upgrade the plan or remove someone first. See [Usage and limits](usage-and-limits).
:::
