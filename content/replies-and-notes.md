---
title: Replies and notes
description: Reply to customers, leave private notes for your team, and send files both ways.
---

The box at the bottom of a conversation has two tabs: **Reply** and **Internal note**. To reply, [take over](handoff) the conversation first. You can add notes at any time.

## Reply to the customer

Write your message and press **Enter**, or **Send reply**. **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. The customer sees your reply in their chat right away. If they closed the page, they see it when they come back.

## Leave an internal note

Switch to **Internal note** to write something only your team sees: a reminder, a question for a colleague, or what you checked. Notes appear in the transcript with a yellow background. Customers never see them.

@clip inbox-note | An internal note for a colleague. The customer doesn't see it.

On a conversation the AI is still handling, **Add note** lets you leave a note without taking over.

## Send and receive files

Press the paperclip to send a photo or a document with your reply: JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF photos, PDFs, and TXT, CSV, DOCX or XLSX files, up to 10 MB. The customer can open or download it in the chat.

@clip inbox-files | Reply with a PDF. It appears in the transcript and in the customer's chat.

Files customers send appear in the transcript too. Photos show as pictures; documents show as files you can open. Only members of your workspace can open them.
